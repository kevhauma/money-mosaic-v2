Career start

The date your working life started, which is not the same as the date your imported history happens to begin. Every panel on the Income page reads from here: the trend chart, the growth figures, the yearly view and the events rail all start at this date, so a stretch of student-era income or a back-imported opening balance stops distorting the growth story. Leave it empty to use your full history.

Income categories ({{ summary() }})

Which of your income categories count as "your income". Unticking one removes it from every figure on the page at once — the trend chart, the growth cards, the yearly totals, the take-home rate and the events rail. Useful for a category that's mostly one-off gifts or refunds, where a good month would otherwise read as sustained growth. A new income category counts by default.

Annual lump sums

A 13th month, vacation pay or a holiday bonus is real income you earned all year, but it lands in one deposit. Ticking a category here spreads each year’s total evenly across that year’s months on the trend chart, so it stops drawing a spike, stops reading as a raise in the growth figures and events rail, and is left out of the take-home rate entirely — that comparison is about your plain monthly wage. Your yearly totals are unchanged either way: smoothing only redistributes. There are two ways to record one, and which you use depends only on how your employer pays it. 1. It arrives as its own transaction, in its own category. Tick that category in the list below. Only categories you count above are offered — smoothing changes how a category is drawn, so there'd be nothing to act on for one you've already excluded. 2. It is paid inside your regular salary deposit. Then there is no separate category to tick, and the list below won't help. Open Salary details instead, find that month, and type the bonus part into its Bonus column. Both do the same thing to the charts. Use whichever matches your payslip — or both, if some years were paid one way and some the other.

Main income category

Which category your salary deposit lands in. This only matters for the second route above — a bonus you typed into Salary details, rather than one with its own category. That figure says "part of this month's deposit was a 13th month", and this setting says which deposit. Without it, the bonus is taken off every income category that paid you that month, in proportion to what each paid — so a freelance invoice or a rent payment in the same month loses a slice of a bonus it never paid, and gets that slice spread across its year instead. Naming your salary category takes the whole bonus off that one. If the bonus is larger than what that category paid that month, the difference still comes off the others, since the deposit must have straddled both. Only categories you count above are offered. If you have one income category, this changes nothing — it is already the only place a bonus can come from.

Chart colours

Gross pay and net income are always drawn together in the "Net vs gross" section, so telling them apart matters. Pick the colour used for every gross-pay series there — the withheld band and the three growth lines. Default follows your theme's own chart palette. This one is appearance only: it changes no figure anywhere.
