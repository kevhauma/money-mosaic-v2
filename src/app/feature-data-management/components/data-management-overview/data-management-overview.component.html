Where your data lives

Everything you import or enter is stored on the Money Mosaic server you are signed in to, in a single SQLite file. Your browser keeps nothing but your session and a few per-browser preferences like your theme — signing in from another browser or device shows you the same figures. Backing that file up is the server operator's job; the export above is your own copy of it.
