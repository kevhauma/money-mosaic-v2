Privacy Blurs every amount on the pages you use to look at your figures — Dashboard, Income, Recurring and Explore — so you can screen-share or let someone glance at your screen without showing your real numbers. Labels, charts and links keep working, and nothing about your data changes. Every one of those pages carries this same toggle in its own header, so you can switch it on and off without coming back here. The pages where you work on your data — Accounts, Transactions, Categories — are deliberately left alone: a blurred balance can't be checked against the bank. Blur amounts on my insight pages
