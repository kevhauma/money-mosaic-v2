# TICKET-ACC-07 — Accounts page shows actual balances, not net worth

- **Area:** Accounts
- **Type:** Feature
- **Traceability:** revises FR-STAT-4 (net-worth-over-time) for the Accounts feature specifically; realigns with FR-ACC-3 (balance = opening balance + Σ transactions) and FR-STAT-1's "displayed balance is still the real bank balance" clause; reopens [TICKET-STAT-03](../../v1.1_joint_accounts/tickets/TICKET-STAT-03-contribution-net-worth.md)'s AC "per-account net-worth-over-time … uses the stake figure … so the per-account lines still sum to the combined line" — that invariant is deliberately dropped here.

## User story

As someone tracking accounts (especially a joint one), I want the Accounts page to show what's actually in each account, so the page answers "what do I have" instead of folding in category kind, nullification, manual attribution, and joint ownership-share adjustments that belong to the net-worth concept, not to an account balance.

## Description

The Accounts page (`feature-accounts`) is meant to be purely about accounts — real balances, one per account. Today its overview chart and the account-detail balance chart instead plot each account's **net-worth contribution** (my "stake"), which [TICKET-STAT-03](../../v1.1_joint_accounts/tickets/TICKET-STAT-03-contribution-net-worth.md) intentionally introduced for the Dashboard's combined net-worth figure and then, per that ticket's own acceptance criteria, reused for this page's per-account chart too, so the stacked total would agree with net worth. That was a deliberate call at the time; this ticket reverses it for the Accounts page only. The account cards on the same page already show the real balance (`AccountsStore.balancesById`) plus a separate "your share" line for joint accounts — the chart is the one place on this page still showing the stake instead of the balance, and it's most visible on a joint account, where "your share" and the charted line can differ substantially from what's actually in the account.

## Current situation (as-is)

- [accounts-overview.component.html:20](../../../src/app/feature-accounts/components/accounts-overview/accounts-overview.component.html) renders `<app-net-worth-history-chart>`, a stacked-area chart, one band per active account.
- [net-worth-history-chart.component.ts](../../../src/app/feature-accounts/components/net-worth-history-chart/net-worth-history-chart.component.ts) sources its series from `balanceTrendSignals` ([balance-trend-signals.ts](../../../src/app/feature-accounts/balance-trend-signals.ts)), which calls `computeAccountBalanceTrends` ([account-balance-trend.ts](../../../src/app/core/stats/account-balance-trend.ts)) — this in turn calls `computeNetWorthTrend` ([net-worth-trend.ts](../../../src/app/core/stats/net-worth-trend.ts)) **per account**, which seeds from `openingBalance × (joint ? ownershipShare : 1)` and walks every transaction through `resolveContribution` ([classify-joint-leg.ts](../../../src/app/core/stats/classify-joint-leg.ts)) — the same contribution-weighting used for the Dashboard's combined net worth: `neutral`-category (partner) inflows count as 0, joint spending is scaled by `ownershipShare`, nullified/manually-attributed transactions follow the override rules, and transfer legs are suppressed/reweighted.
- The **account-detail** page's own chart ([account-balance-chart.component.ts](../../../src/app/feature-accounts/components/account-balance-chart/account-balance-chart.component.ts)) shares the exact same `balanceTrendSignals` helper, so it has the identical mismatch for a single account.
- By contrast, [accounts.store.ts:63](../../../src/app/core/state/accounts.store.ts) already computes and exposes the **real** balance untouched by any of this: `balancesById` = `openingBalance + Σ transaction.amount`, explicitly commented "unchanged by the contribution model — the accounts UI keeps showing this figure for every account, joint included (TICKET-STAT-03)". `accounts-overview.component.ts`'s `accountCards()` reads `balancesById` for each card's headline balance, and `jointAccountStakeById` separately for the card's "your share" line — the two numbers are already kept apart on the cards; only the chart conflates them.
- This was not an oversight: [TICKET-STAT-03](../../v1.1_joint_accounts/tickets/TICKET-STAT-03-contribution-net-worth.md)'s acceptance criteria explicitly required the per-account chart to "use the stake figure for joint accounts so the per-account lines still sum to the combined line" — a deliberate choice this ticket reverses for the Accounts feature.
- The Dashboard's own net worth ([net-worth-header.component.ts](../../../src/app/feature-dashboard/components/net-worth-header/net-worth-header.component.ts), `StatsStore.netWorthTrend()`) is a **separate** code path (`computeNetWorthTrend` called directly with the full account list, not per-account) and is unaffected either way — net worth as a concept keeps living on the Dashboard.

## Desired result (to-be)

- A new pure helper in `core/stats` (e.g. `computeAccountBalanceHistory`) walks a running total from **raw** `openingBalance + Σ transaction.amount` per bucket, per account — the same maths as `AccountsStore.balancesById`, extended over time. It takes no `JointLegContext` and calls no `resolveContribution`/`classifyJointLeg`: category kind (including `neutral`), a transaction's `nullified` flag, `attributionOverride`, and `ownershipShare` are all irrelevant to what gets plotted.
- `computeAccountBalanceTrends` (consumed by both the accounts-overview stacked chart and the account-detail chart) switches to this new helper in place of `computeNetWorthTrend`. `computeNetWorthTrend` itself is untouched — the Dashboard's combined net-worth trend keeps calling it exactly as today.
- The Accounts-overview stacked chart's top edge becomes **total real balance across active accounts**, not combined net worth — a joint account's band is its full balance, matching the number on its own card, not its "your share" line. TICKET-STAT-03's "stack sums to net worth" invariant no longer holds for this page by design; it still holds for the Dashboard.
- The account-detail balance chart plots that one account's real balance over time, matching the balance shown at the top of the same page.
- Chart copy on the Accounts page (title, any legend/tooltip label reading "net worth") is relabelled to describe balances, not net worth, so the page doesn't visually claim a concept it no longer shows (e.g. `app-net-worth-history-chart` → renamed component/selector, or at minimum its rendered heading text — implementer's call on how far the rename goes, see Notes).
- Drill-down/click-through behaviour (clicking a band/point routes to that account or its transactions) is unchanged in mechanism, just now backed by real-balance data.

## Notes

- Deliberately scoped to `feature-accounts` only. Net worth as a concept (contribution-weighted, joint-aware) keeps living on the Dashboard exactly as TICKET-STAT-03 shipped it — this ticket does not touch `computeNetWorthTrend`, `StatsStore`, or anything under `feature-dashboard/`.
- How far to take the rename (component/selector `net-worth-history-chart` → something like `account-balance-history-chart`, vs. a smaller copy-only change keeping the existing file/selector names) is an implementation call — either is acceptable as long as no page-visible copy says "net worth" where a balance is now shown. If the component is renamed, update its imports in `accounts-overview.component.ts` and its spec file accordingly.
- This is the mirror image of [TICKET-STAT-03](../../v1.1_joint_accounts/tickets/TICKET-STAT-03-contribution-net-worth.md)'s own explicit trade-off: that ticket accepted the stake diverging from the real balance as a deliberate "IOU" concept for net worth. This ticket says that trade-off shouldn't leak onto a page whose entire purpose is showing accounts as they actually are.
- Filed under v1.6 at the user's request (the currently active version), even though the change is entirely within `feature-accounts` and unrelated to income-growth — no dependency on any v1.6/FR-INC ticket.
